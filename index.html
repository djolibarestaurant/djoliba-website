Our Story

Guinea on a Plate

DJOLIBA — named after the Mandinka word for the mighty Niger River — was born from a deep love of Guinean culture, community, and cuisine. We bring the authentic tastes of Conakry directly to Philadelphia at 6735 Elmwood Ave.

Every dish we serve is made with time-honored recipes, fresh ingredients, and the spices that define Guinean cooking. Whether you grew up eating Riz Gras on Sundays or are discovering Poulet DG for the very first time — you are welcome here.
